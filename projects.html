CourseCast — Course Registration Forecasting (In Progress)

Click Here to Learn More!

A team project (7 people) building a "weather forecast for course registration" at UIUC — given a student's registration time ticket and desired sections, CourseCast predicts the probability each section will still be open, using an hourly LightGBM hazard model trained on live snapshots of the Course Explorer API. I'm serving as the team's Evaluator: defining what a well-calibrated forecast means, measuring model performance against a historical-baseline comparator with calibration metrics (Brier score, reliability diagrams), and authoring the public dataset documentation following the "Datasheets for Datasets" framework. For the rest of the semester, I'll be running ablation studies to isolate how much the course-description text embeddings actually help forecasts for brand-new courses with no registration history.

  • Model Evaluation
  • Calibration & Brier Score
  • LightGBM
  • Python
  • pandas
  • Ablation Studies
  • Dataset Documentation

Quantum Chemistry on IBM Quantum Hardware (In Progress)

Click Here to Learn More!

A quantum computing research project investigating how variational quantum algorithms can simulate molecules at higher accuracy than classical methods allow. Molecular states are mapped onto qubits and evolved with a hybrid quantum-classical workflow — VQE, VQD, and QSR — to find molecular energies, a workload designed to run on today's noisy IBM quantum hardware. The goal is to run the final programs on IBM's quantum computers and demonstrate an improvement over published literature results for a sufficiently complex molecule.

Currently working through: finding better ansatzes/representations for molecules and testing different optimizers, applying error mitigation for real IBM quantum runtime, benchmarking against classical quantum chemistry baselines (Hartree-Fock, DFT), and extending to more advanced literature methods (SQD, ADAPT-VQE) as the core pipeline matures.

  • Variational Algorithms (VQE/VQD)
  • Qiskit
  • IBM Quantum
  • Python
  • Quantum Chemistry
  • Error Mitigation
  • Hartree-Fock / DFT

Charcot: AI-Powered Copilot for Psychiatric Assessments

Click Here to Learn More!

For this hackathon project, I worked with a team of four to build Charcot, a multimodal AI copilot for neuropsychiatric diagnosis that provides real-time diagnostic support during live patient interviews. The platform analyzes both conversational and nonverbal cues to help psychiatrists catch diagnostic patterns they might miss. I contributed real-time speech-to-text transcription, facial landmark detection with MediaPipe for behavioral analysis, and custom algorithms for eye-contact monitoring, gaze stability tracking, and breathing-rate estimation — all on a privacy-first architecture with 100% local video processing.
